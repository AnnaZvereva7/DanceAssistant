Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 31, 2026

DanceAssistant is used to manage dance lessons, bookings, schedules, payments and related communication between students and trainers. This page explains what information the application may process and how that information is used.

Information We Process

  • Account information such as your name and email address.
  • Lesson and schedule information such as dates, times, trainers, students and lesson status.
  • Payment-related records used to track balances and completed payments.
  • Optional contact and communication data such as Telegram account identifiers when you connect messaging features.
  • Calendar-related information when lesson invitations or availability are synchronized.

How Information Is Used

  • To authenticate users and provide access to the appropriate parts of the application.
  • To create, update, confirm, cancel, and display lessons and schedules.
  • To calculate balances and record payment activity.
  • To send service messages such as lesson invitations, reminders, or updates by email or connected messaging tools.
  • To connect students and trainers for booking and lesson management.

Third-Party Services

The application may use third-party services such as Google sign-in, Google Calendar, email delivery services, and Telegram integration. When these features are used, relevant data may be processed by those services according to their own policies.

Data Storage

Information entered into the application may be stored in the application database and used for ongoing lesson and account management. Session data may also be stored to keep you signed in securely while you use the service.

Data Sharing

Information is intended to be available only to the people and roles required to operate the service, such as the relevant student, trainer, or administrator. Data is not intended to be shared publicly except where a feature is explicitly designed for public booking access.

Your Choices

  • You may choose whether to provide optional data such as messaging details where supported.
  • You may request updates to your personal details through the application.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or about your data in the application, contact danceassistantnl@gmail.com.
